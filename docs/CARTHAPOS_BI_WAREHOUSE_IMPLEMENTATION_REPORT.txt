7. KNOWN LIMITATIONS (source-data, not ETL defects)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - products.csv stock = 0 everywhere -> inventory is a point-in-time snapshot;
    v_inventory_status surfaces INVENTORY_DATA_NOT_AVAILABLE. Not an ETL bug.
  - kitchen_orders.csv has started_at == ready_at on ~66% of rows (fabricated
    test data) -> prep_minutes ~ 0; ETL stores timestamps faithfully.
  - sale_items.csv line totals (119,854) do not reconcile to sales.csv totals
    (139,869.52): line_total is exactly unit_price * quantity and the sale
    totals carry extra (VAT/fees/discounts) not present at item level. This
    exists in the source CSVs; ETL stores both grains faithfully. Per-product
    revenue (v_product_performance) reflects the honest item grain.
  - Hour-of-day is stored as an integer transactionHour (11 distinct hours
    used). Naive POS timestamps are not timezone-converted.
  - suppliers.csv is empty and no appointments are exported for the restaurant
    business type -> v_suppliers / v_appointments stay empty by design.
