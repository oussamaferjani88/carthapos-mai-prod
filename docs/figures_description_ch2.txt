FIGURE 1: fig:market-solution-landscape
-----------------------------------------------------------
Type: Conceptual Landscape Diagram
Filename: figures/ch2-market-solution-landscape.png
Caption: Conceptual landscape of solution categories relevant to CarthaPOS.

Description:
Draw a conceptual landscape/positioning diagram showing the relationship
between different solution categories in the POS and business software
market, and where CarthaPOS fits.

The diagram should show THREE main zones or regions, arranged as overlapping
circles or grouped boxes:

ZONE 1 - Traditional POS Systems:
  - Off-the-shelf POS applications (e.g. Square, Lightspeed, Toast)
  - Characteristics: fixed features, per-terminal licensing, no generation
  - Limitation: not customizable per sector, no centralized management

ZONE 2 - Centralized Administration Platforms:
  - Multi-tenant SaaS admin panels
  - Characteristics: client/license management, module control, role-based access
  - Limitation: no POS generation, no BI integration

ZONE 3 - CarthaPOS (the intersection / highlighted center):
  - Combines both: centralized administration + automatic POS generation + BI
  - Unique value: generates customized Electron POS per client sector,
    manages licenses with Ed25519 signatures, integrates a full BI pipeline
    (ETL, warehouse, Metabase dashboards)

OPTIONAL ZONE 4 - Standalone BI Tools:
  - Metabase, Power BI, Tableau as standalone solutions
  - Characteristics: visualization only, no POS generation, no tenant isolation
  - CarthaPOS differentiator: embeds Metabase with per-tenant filtering

Use arrows or a Venn-style layout to show that CarthaPOS sits at the
intersection of POS generation, centralized administration, and business
intelligence - a position not occupied by any single existing solution.

Color coding:
  - Gray/light for existing solution categories
  - Green or highlighted for CarthaPOS
